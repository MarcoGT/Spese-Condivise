Shared Expenses is the simple app to split costs with whoever you want — on a trip, at home, anywhere.

Create a sheet, add people, and start logging expenses. The app automatically calculates balances and tells you exactly who needs to pay whom.

KEY FEATURES
• Shared sheets via iCloud — invite anyone with a link
• Real-time balances — updated instantly with every expense
• Settlement suggestions — who pays whom and how much
• Categories — groceries, transport, restaurants and more
• Statistics — total, average per person, expenses by category
• Multi-currency — with customizable exchange rate
• Export to PDF — full summary ready to share
• Expense archive — keep history without cluttering the main view

No sign-up, no account. Just you, your people, and the expenses to split.
