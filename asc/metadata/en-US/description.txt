Split expenses with friends, roommates and family — no signup required.

No account, no third-party servers: your sheets sync through your own iCloud and your data stays yours. Apple confirms it: "Data Not Collected".

Create a sheet, add people and log expenses. The app works out the balances automatically and tells you exactly who owes whom.

PERFECT FOR
• Group trips and vacations
• Roommates and household bills
• Dinners, nights out and shared gifts
• Families who want to know what a trip really cost

FEATURES
• Shared sheets via iCloud — invite anyone with a link
• Live balances, updated with every expense
• Settle-up suggestions: who pays whom, and how much
• Categories: groceries, transport, restaurants and more
• Statistics: total, average per person, spending by category
• Multi-currency with custom exchange rates
• Export a PDF summary to share
• Settle up and start fresh without losing your history
• Home Screen widget to see your balance at a glance

Simple, private, no ads.
